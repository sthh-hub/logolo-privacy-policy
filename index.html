Privacy Policy

Logolo Creator Intelligence · Operated by Logolo International Inc.
Effective date: September 24, 2026 · Contact: sherrytsai@logolo.ca

Logolo International Inc. ("Logolo", "we", "us") operates Logolo Creator Intelligence, an analytics platform that helps brands discover and evaluate Instagram creators for potential collaboration. This Privacy Policy explains what data our application accesses through Meta's APIs, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Data We Access via Meta APIs

When you connect your Instagram professional account or Facebook Page to Logolo Creator Intelligence, or when you use our creator discovery features, our app may access the following categories of data through Meta's APIs, strictly within the permissions you grant:

  • Account information: Instagram business/creator account identifiers, username, profile name, biography, profile picture URL, and account type.
  • Audience and content metrics: follower counts and aggregated audience insights (such as age ranges, gender distribution, and top cities/countries), media objects you have published, and engagement metrics (likes, comments, reach, impressions) on that content.
  • Creator discovery data: publicly available information about Instagram creators surfaced through Meta's Creator Marketplace discovery endpoints, used to match creators with brand briefs.
  • Messaging metadata: where you authorize outreach features, metadata about conversations initiated through Meta's messaging products (such as timestamps and delivery status). We do not use message content for advertising or sell it.

We do not access your Facebook password, private messages unrelated to the features you enable, or data outside the permissions you explicitly grant.

2. How We Use the Data

  • To provide creator search, matching scores (relevance, engagement quality, audience fit), and analytics dashboards.
  • To display aggregated insights about your own connected accounts.
  • To operate outreach workflows you initiate (e.g., sending collaboration invitations).
  • To maintain the security and integrity of our platform and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use data obtained from Meta APIs for advertising targeting outside the purposes described here, and we do not share it with data brokers.

3. Data Sharing

We share data only as necessary to operate the service:

  • Service providers: hosting and infrastructure providers that process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal compliance: where required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.

4. Data Retention

We retain API-derived data only for as long as needed to provide the service or as required by law. Aggregated, de-identified analytics may be retained longer. If you disconnect your Instagram account or Facebook Page, or request deletion, we delete associated personal data within 30 days, except where retention is legally required.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under Canada's PIPEDA and, where applicable, the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, contact us at sherrytsai@logolo.ca. You may also disconnect your account at any time from within the app or via your Meta account settings, which revokes our API access.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls limited to authorized personnel, and audit logging of API access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Children's Privacy

Logolo Creator Intelligence is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
